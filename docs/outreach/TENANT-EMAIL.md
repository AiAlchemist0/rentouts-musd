**Subject:** Pay rent without selling your bitcoin (testnet pilot — no real money)

Hi `[first name]`,

If you hold BTC and pay rent, every month starts the same way: sell some bitcoin to make the dollar payment happen.

RentOuts is a different shape. On Mezo testnet, you borrow **MUSD** — a Bitcoin-backed dollar — against your own BTC, right inside the app, and prepay your deposit and rent into a **non-custodial escrow**. The escrow releases rent to your landlord one period at a time; you can never be late, and they can never take it early. At move-out the deposit goes straight back to you — no forms, no persuasion. Your BTC never leaves your own trove: the landlord can't touch it, we can't touch it, and you can repay the loan and take it back whenever you like.

Everything above already runs on testnet. We're looking for the first outside tenants to try it: **one testnet lease, about five minutes, no real money** — the demo landlord rents to anyone with a single wallet. Then tell us what confused you; that's the whole ask.
