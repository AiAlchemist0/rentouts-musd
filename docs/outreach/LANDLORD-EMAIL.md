**Subject:** Rent that arrives on day one — a testnet pilot (no real money)

Hi `[first name]`,

Two questions every long-term landlord knows: *when* will the rent actually land, and *what shape* will the deposit be in at move-out?

We built RentOuts to answer both. It runs on Mezo, a Bitcoin blockchain, and works like this: the tenant borrows a Bitcoin-backed dollar (MUSD) against their own BTC and **prepays the deposit and every month's rent into a non-custodial escrow** before moving in. Rent unlocks to you one period at a time — you can never be paid late, because you were paid on day one. At move-out the deposit returns to the tenant automatically. The escrow has no admin key, takes no fee, and RentOuts never holds the funds.

What we'd ask of you: run **one testnet lease** with us. Ten minutes, no real money, faucet tokens only. You list a lease, we play the tenant, you watch rent release each period and the deposit return at the end — then you tell us what a real landlord would need before trusting it with a real lease. We allowlist your wallet first (a compliance step on the lease-share contract); just reply with your wallet address or write to `[contact]`.
