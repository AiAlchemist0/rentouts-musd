**First line:** RentOuts is live on Mezo testnet — pay rent and deposits in MUSD without selling your BTC.

Rent paid in MUSD, bitcoin stays bitcoin. That's the whole idea:

- The tenant borrows MUSD against their own BTC **in the app** and prepays deposit + rent into a **non-custodial escrow** — no selling.
- Rent releases to the landlord period by period. Nobody can take it early; nobody can be late.
- At move-out the deposit goes straight back to the tenant. The escrow has no admin key and takes no fee, and the BTC stays in the tenant's own trove the whole time.

New in Wave 2 (`RentEscrowV2` — deployed and verified on Sourcify, app integration next): **borrow-and-pay in one transaction**, **mutual early termination** with an agreed penalty, and a **dispute timeout** so nobody can hold a lease's funds hostage.

Testnet only — faucet BTC, no real money anywhere. Try it with one wallet: the demo landlord rents to anyone → https://mezo.rentouts.co

We're looking for **first tenants and first landlords** to run a testnet lease and tell us what's confusing (5 questions, ~3 minutes: `[form link]`). Landlords need an allowlist on the lease-share contract — ask here or at `[contact]`.
